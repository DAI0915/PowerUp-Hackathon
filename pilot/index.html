PowerUP pilot · SCE Cuthbert circuit · Malibu

456 homes behind switch RAR8848

Our company buys Base Core batteries and puts them free in homes that volunteer to share with neighbors. On ordinary days the batteries earn money. When SCE shuts the power off, the switch opens and the homes behind it run as one island on those batteries and rooftop solar. This page shows what the batteries earn, what holding them full costs, and how many homes they would have kept on through the 16 real shutoffs that cut these homes.

How this is worked out

Sources and limits
  • Real: SCE's Cuthbert line, RAR8848's 456 customers, and every Cuthbert shutoff from 2019 to January 2025 (SCE shutoff records). Whole-circuit breaker cuts and RAR8848 opened alone both cut these homes; 16 events, 435 hours.
  • Real: what a Base Core (39.2 kWh, 20 kW, 20% floor) earns trading CAISO day-ahead prices for SCE, Oct to Dec 2025, with perfect foresight: $0.91 a day. Yearly figures repeat this quarter; summer spreads are usually wider.
  • Real: Malibu has 7.1 home batteries (median 20 kWh) and 8.4 solar-only roofs per 100 homes (SCE interconnection records). Medical Baseline is 2.6% of homes (SCE's Dec 2024 report on Maguire to the CPUC). SCE notified 509,245 customers and cut 152,393 in its Jan 20 2025 event. The Emergency Load Reduction Program pays $2 per kWh.
  • Real: lead times are from the Bryson Fire, Oct 2026. The first Santa Ana signal came 6.5 days before the shutoff, and SCE named Cuthbert 3 days before.
  • Estimated: where RAR8848 sits, and where each home is (spread evenly along the line). Each home's battery, solar, medical need and choice is drawn at the real rates, so a dot is a stand-in, not an address. 60% of battery owners join; 90% of homes without a battery subscribe for emergency power at $9.99 a month; medical homes always join, free. In a shutoff everyone on the island pays 2x SCE's top TOU price ($0.60 assumed) per kWh; sellers get that less SCE's $0.20 delivery (assumed); solar roofs sell all they make. Every home that receives keeps critical loads on (8 kWh a day). A roof makes 27 kWh a day.
  • Not modeled yet: SCE approval to island its line, Base firmware for sharing, battery wear, and what volunteers pay. The island also needs one battery certified to form the grid.
