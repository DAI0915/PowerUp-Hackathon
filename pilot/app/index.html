        The pilot business: free Base batteries behind RAR8848

        Our company buys Base Core batteries and places them free in homes that volunteer to share with neighbors. On ordinary days the fleet trades; before a shutoff it holds full under PowerUP's ladder above; when SCE opens RAR8848, the 456 homes behind it run as one island on the batteries and rooftop solar, with one battery certified to form the grid. The stretch is last in its series (breaker, then RCS0036, then RAR8848), sits on the coastal flats 9 km from the Bryson burn, and was opened alone only once while the Cuthbert breaker cut the whole circuit 15 times, so these homes are mostly collateral.

        Sources and data notes

          Caveats
          • SCE publishes circuit load without units. The numbers fit amps per phase at 16 kV, so 1 unit is read as about 27.7 kW three-phase. That is an inference, shown as such.
          • Serra's load profile is near zero and unusable; the app uses Merlin's shape scaled by customers.
          • Tuna has no SCE line or customer count on record: 500 customers and a coastal band are assumed. Seaboard is drawn for context only.
          • The coast is hand-traced and approximate. Lines are SCE's, simplified to about 10 m.
          • Restoration has not happened. Times after Oct 17 20:00 are planned, not actual.
          • SCE does not publish Medical Baseline counts per circuit. The Medical Baseline homes here are demo homes placed by a seeded random draw.
          • Money uses real CAISO day-ahead prices for the SCE zone on the same dates in 2025 (wholesale value of one battery's best daily schedule, perfect foresight). A California home battery does not collect the wholesale price directly today, and no Malibu node publishes a price. The assumed TOU rates are the alternative in the drawer.
          • Base has no California fleet. This is a demo of what a dispatcher could do, not a record of what happened.
