Interlock sits between a home-battery fleet's software and the batteries. Every order from the optimizer, a market bid or a utility event passes ordered checks: shape, signature, freshness, precedence, each home's backup floor, the inverter, the circuit, then size and ramp. Each check answers on the gentlest rung that keeps the rules, and Interlock can only shrink, slow or stop an order. It never counts a quiet battery as capacity. This replays CAISO's record-peak evening on four real SCE circuits, guard off against guard on.

Responsible use

Interlock could be used to throttle or lock owners out of their own batteries, or become a single point of attack. It can only shrink or stop commands, never below a home's own backup, falls back to each battery's local safe plan if it fails, and logs every hold where the owner can see it.
